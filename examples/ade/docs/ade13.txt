A 5-month-old infant became lethargic and poorly responsive after receiving 1 drop of brimonidine in each eye. An 11-day-old infant became lethargic and apneic after a single drop of brimonidine. Apparent central nervous system depression in infants after the use of topical brimonidine. CONCLUSIONS: Topical brimonidine may be associated with central nervous system depression in infants. PURPOSE: To report two cases in which topical brimonidine resulted in apparent central nervous system depression and unresponsiveness in an infant.
